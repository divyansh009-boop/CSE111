01 THE PROBLEM

A public tap should never become a silent leak.

One overlooked tap.
A chain reaction.

In busy schools, colleges, parks and community spaces, taps are easily left running. The water keeps moving. No one notices.

JalRakshak is a gentle interruption: a compact sensing system that draws attention at exactly the moment it matters.
